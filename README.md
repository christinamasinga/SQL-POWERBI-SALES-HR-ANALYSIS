# Sales Performance, Customer Segmentation, and HR Analytics

## Project Overview
This project analyzes three main areas of a business: retail sales performance, customer subscription behavior, and human resources (HR) employee data. The goal is to extract key business insights to help the company make better decisions regarding sales, customer retention, and employee management.

## ️ Process
The analysis was completed in the following steps:
1. Data Cleaning: The raw Excel and CSV files were cleaned to fix formatting errors and remove blank rows.
2. Database Setup: The cleaned data was imported into SQL Server.
3. SQL Analysis: SQL queries were written to answer 16 specific business questions (aggregations, filtering, and calculations).
4. Data Visualization: Power BI was used to create interactive dashboards to visualize the SQL findings.
5. Documentation: All results and insights were documented in a comprehensive report.

Key Results & Insights

Sales & Customer Insights:
* Revenue Drivers: Basic subscription plans contribute the largest portion of total revenue, showing that affordable options are highly popular.
* Regional Balance: Customers are evenly distributed across all regions (East, West, North, South), indicating good market penetration.
* Customer Retention: The company has a healthy number of active subscriptions compared to canceled ones.

HR & Employee Insights:
* Department Income:The Sales department has the highest average monthly income, likely due to performance-based bonuses.
* Overtime Work:The majority of employees working overtime are male, which may indicate a need to review workload distribution.
* Attrition:Different departments show varying attrition rates, highlighting specific areas where employee retention strategies are needed.

Conclusion & Recommendations
The data shows that the company has a strong customer base driven by basic subscription plans. To grow further, the company should focus on retaining these basic customers and offering them incentives to upgrade to Premium plans. 

On the HR side, management should investigate the high overtime hours among male employees and implement targeted retention programs for departments with high turnover rates. Overall, using data-driven insights will help the company improve both customer satisfaction and employee well-being.
